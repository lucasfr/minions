## What the YASA algorithm does (and the old mrpheus did not)

- Band-pass: `mne.filter.filter_data` FIR (Hamming, zero phase, 0.3-1.5 Hz, 0.2 Hz transition bands, `reflect_limited` padding) on the continuous signal, then stage mask on the peaks.
- Peaks: `scipy.signal.find_peaks` on the negative and the positive signal with height limits (trough 40-200, peak 10-150 uV), plateaus at their middle; each negative peak takes the closest following positive peak (`searchsorted`); a fake positive peak is appended after the last negative peak if needed; peak-to-peak 75-350 exclusive.
- Phase durations from the zero crossings (`x > 0` changes) around each peak; conditions on the edges, on `sw_dur == neg + pos` (both rounded to 4 decimals), on duration and phase limits (exclusive), and a positive slope.
- **Found by comparison, not by reading:** after the criteria YASA drops every event sharing its `Start`, then every event sharing its `End`, with another event (`drop_duplicates(keep=False)`), so two negative peaks paired with one positive peak both vanish. My first port gave 75 events against YASA's 73 on one run; instrumenting the real function (good_sw total 75, returned 73) located it.
- Quirk reproduced: a peak before the first zero crossing indexes `zc[-1]` in Python (wraps to the last crossing). Equivalent in effect to not wrapping (rejected either way), kept for fidelity.
- Not implemented: YASA's coupling with spindles and `remove_outliers`.

## Mutation checks (each deviation put back, tests must fail)

13 mutants: 11 caught. Initially 8 of 13; the crafted signal (with deliberate boundary pairs: peak-to-peak 75 and 350, phase durations 0.3, 1.5, 0.1, 1.0 s, exact zeros, plateaus, double-humped waves) caught three more.

Caught: no removal of shared start/end; transition 0.25 Hz; Hann window in the FIR; pairing with the previous positive peak; stage mask not applied to peaks; no trough/peak limits; filter per epoch; inclusive peak-to-peak limit; inclusive negative-duration limit; duration-equality check removed; zero crossings by `x >= 0`.

Not caught, both equivalent (change nothing): (1) removing only the shared-`End` check: when `sw_dur == neg + pos` holds, a shared end implies a shared start; (2) no wrap-around at the first crossing: the event is rejected either way.
